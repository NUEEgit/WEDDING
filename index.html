ॐ गणानां त्वा गणपतिं हवामहे
कविं कवीनामुपमश्रवस्तमम् ।
ज्येष्ठराजं ब्रह्मणां ब्रह्मणस्पत

jyeṣṭharājaṁ brahmaṇāṁ brahmaṇas pata, ā naḥ śṛṇvann ūtibhiḥ sīda sādanam.

We invoke you, O Ganapati, wisest among the wise, foremost of all. Come, gracious lord, hear us, and take your seat among us as we begin this auspicious undertaking.

Rigveda 2.23.1
